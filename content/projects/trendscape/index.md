##### About

Trendscape is a toolkit for exploring inter-concept relationships on language model latent spaces. It combines word embedding exploration, optimal transport distance metrics, and visualization to analyze how concepts relate and evolve.

Applied to organizational alignment analysis in collaboration with Resonac — measuring gaps between management vision and frontline perspectives using concept clustering and PCA-based visualization.

- **YANS 2024** [S5-P02] — 単語分散表現モデルの埋め込み空間を用いた概念間探索手法の構築と大規模言語モデルの機械論的解釈可能性への応用
- **NLP 2025** [P2-25] — Trendscape 1.0: 言語モデルの潜在空間上の概念探索
