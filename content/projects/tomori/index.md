##### About

[Tomori](https://tomori.care) is a conversational AI companion developed by [Re-MENTIA](https://rementia.jp/) for dementia self-support. It learns about users through repeated interaction and facilitates family connection by sharing photos, reminders, and warm messages via LINE.

##### Role

Founding member of Re-MENTIA (joined March 2025). Lead developer of Tomori.

- **[IPA 未踏アドバンスト 2025上期](https://www.ipa.go.jp/jinzai/mitou/advanced/2025first/gaiyou-is-1.html)** — 「認知症向け仮想ヘルパーの開発と自立支援プラットフォームの創出」石黒浩PM，採択・修了．
